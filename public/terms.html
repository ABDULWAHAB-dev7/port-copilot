What the tools are

Customs Copilot drafts replies to customs officer questions and checks port or CFS invoices against a tariff card. Both are aids for a licensed professional. They are not legal advice, a customs declaration, or a filing.

Responsibility

A licensed professional remains responsible for anything filed, sent, paid or declared. You must check every draft and every flag against the source documents before you act on it. Approving a draft in the tool does not send it anywhere, and copying it does not file it.

Sample data

The shipments, questions, tariff cards and invoices in the tools are synthetic. They are provided for demonstration and testing only.

No warranty

The tools are provided as they are. The operator does not promise that drafts or charge checks are complete, correct or accepted by any customs authority or terminal.

Acceptable use

  • Do not enter real personal, customer or shipment data unless the operator has confirmed in writing that this is allowed.
  • Do not use the tools to produce documents that you do not have authority to file.
  • Do not attempt to overload, probe or interfere with the service.
